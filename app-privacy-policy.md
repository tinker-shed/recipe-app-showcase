# RecipeApp — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 7 November 2026_

RecipeApp is a personal, offline recipe library for iPhone and iPad. This policy
explains how it handles your information. In short: it doesn't collect any.

## What RecipeApp does with your data

- Your recipes, photos, notes, favourites and cooking history are stored only on
  your own device.
- If you use the RecipeApp Mac companion, your library can be synced to your
  iPhone and iPad over your local network (Wi-Fi). That sync happens directly
  between your own devices and is not routed through any server.

## What RecipeApp does not do

- It does not collect, store or transmit any personal data to the developer or to
  any third party.
- It has no accounts and no sign-in.
- It contains no adverts, no analytics and no tracking.
- It does not use the cloud. Nothing leaves your devices except the direct,
  on-device sync described above.

## Permissions

- **Local Network** is used only to sync your library between your own Mac,
  iPhone and iPad when you ask it to. It is not used for any other purpose.

## Children's privacy

RecipeApp does not collect data from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.

## Contact

RecipeApp is a personal project. Questions can be raised at the project page:
https://github.com/tinker-shed/recipe-app-showcase

Because the app collects and transmits no data, there is nothing to request,
correct or delete.
